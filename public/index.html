About the college

Superior College Bhakkar is a two-year intermediate college serving students across the district. Classes stay small enough that teachers know every student by name, and the subject lineup covers both pre-medical and computer science tracks — so students can choose a path that fits where they're headed next.

Beyond the classroom, the college keeps a full calendar of student life — from a sports gala to a farewell for graduating batches — because two years of college should be remembered for more than just exams.
